Where earth meets sky in endless white,
Two elements bond, invisible might.

Salt (NaCl) is composed of sodium (Na) and chlorine (Cl). When separated, these elements are highly reactive.

Sodium is a soft, silvery metal that reacts violently with water. Chlorine is a toxic greenish-yellow gas.

Together, they form common table salt - essential for life and completely safe to consume.

Hint: Click the crystal to break it, then drag both elements to the drop zone to form NaCl.
