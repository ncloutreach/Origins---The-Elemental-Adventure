The game integrates scientific inquiry with experiential learning. Players explore natural and human-made environments, uncover raw materials, and solve puzzles that represent real-world processes (e.g., electrolysis for hydrogen and oxygen, mining for iron, fertilizers for nitrogen). The mechanics of interaction—like splitting water molecules, crafting glass from sand, or connecting electrical circuits—mirror actual scientific phenomena. This approach combines hands-on conceptual learning with storytelling, making abstract chemistry concepts concrete and memorable.
