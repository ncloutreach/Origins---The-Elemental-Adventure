Players begin as Samir, an inquisitive adventurer who receives a mysterious alchemist’s journal. The journal contains riddles about elements and their natural sources. Players must solve these riddles, visit unique environments, and collect elements while learning about their origins.

Level 1 – Whispering Woods (Carbon):

Guided by the clue, “This was once a major fuel for trains,” players uncover coal in the forest. On collecting coal (a form of carbon), the game explains carbon’s role as the building block of life.

Level 2 – Silver Lake (Hydrogen & Oxygen):

Players arrive at a lake with the hint, “The water holds two life-giving elements.” They must assemble an electrolysis device to split water molecules, unlocking hydrogen and oxygen.

Level 3 – Mountain Forge (Iron):

At a mountain forge, players interact with blacksmiths and extract iron ore. A mini-puzzle of collecting ore and forging tools unlocks iron.

Level 4 – Sunny Orchard (Nitrogen):

In an orchard, the clue reads, “The unseen element that helps life grow.” Players nurture trees using the right amount of fertilizer in a logic puzzle, revealing nitrogen.

Level 5 – Coastal Cave (Calcium):

Inside a seaside cave with shells and limestone, players solve a pattern-matching puzzle to unlock calcium.

Level 6 – Electric Workshop (Copper):

Players explore a workshop with wires and machines. By connecting circuits in the right order to power the room, they collect copper.

Level 7 – Salt Flats (Sodium & Chlorine):

On a vast salt flat, the journal states, “Two elements in the salt you eat.” Breaking apart crystals and solving a chemistry puzzle unlocks sodium and chlorine.

Level 8 – City Streets (Silicon):

In a modern city, players find silicon in sand and glass. A crafting mechanic (turning sand into glass) confirms its collection.

Final Stage – Assembling the Elements:

After collecting all elements, players return to the central hub. They must place each element in its correct spot on a periodic table and match it with its real-life use. Completing the table unlocks the final journal entry and ending sequence, explaining how these elements interconnect in nature and human life.
